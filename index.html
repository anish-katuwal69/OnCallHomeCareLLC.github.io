How we help at home

Our caregivers support daily living. Every plan is built around the person, their routines and what matters to them.

Personal care

Help with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting and moving safely around the home.

Companionship

Conversation, games, walks and outings, so days feel less long and less lonely.

Meal preparation

Planning and cooking meals that fit the person’s tastes and any diet their doctor has set.

Light housekeeping

Laundry, dishes, tidying and changing bed linens, so the home stays safe and comfortable.

Medication reminders

Reminders to take medicine on time. Our caregivers do not give medication or provide medical care.

Respite for family

Short breaks for family caregivers, from a few hours to a full day, so you can rest knowing someone is there.

Getting started is simple

Most families can go from first call to first visit in a few days.

  1. Call or send a request

    Tell us who needs care and what’s been hard lately.

  2. Meet at home

    We visit to learn about routines, safety and preferences.

  3. Agree on a care plan

    Together we set the tasks, the schedule and who to call with questions.

  4. Care begins

    Your caregiver arrives, and we check in regularly to adjust the plan.

Common questions

What is non-medical home care?

It’s help with daily living, such as bathing, dressing, meals, housework and companionship. It does not include nursing, wound care, injections or giving medication.

How do you pay for care?

Families can pay privately. We are completing enrollment as a Qualified Service Provider with North Dakota Health and Human Services. Call us for the current status of Medicaid and waiver-funded care.

How many hours can we book?

That depends on what the person needs. We’ll talk through options during your first call and home visit.

Can we meet the caregiver first?

Yes. We want the person receiving care to feel comfortable, and we’ll make changes if the match isn’t right.
