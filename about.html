About us

A local agency built on a simple idea: care should come to the person, not the other way around.

Who we are

On Call Home Care LLC is a North Dakota home care agency founded in 2026 by Anish Katuwal. We help older adults and people with disabilities live safely and comfortably at home.

Our team has experience working with families, case managers and community programs in eastern North Dakota, and we understand how confusing it can be to find help. We keep things clear and personal.

What we believe

  • People do best in the place they know.
  • Dignity and choice come first in every task.
  • Families deserve straight answers and steady communication.
  • Language and culture matter in care. We speak English, Hindi and Nepali.
